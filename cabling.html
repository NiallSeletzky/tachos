Cabling & Patch Management

Connect and verify the infrastructure.

Proposed lifecycle mapping. The hero names are retained from the artwork. These are overlapping work areas, not five mandatory sequential stages. Project Delivery spans the lifecycle; documentation starts during planning and continues throughout.

Activities

  • Confirm routes, port assignments, cable types and approved connection schedule.
  • Install and identify cabling using approved site and manufacturer practices.
  • Inspect and test against the agreed acceptance plan; record results and faults.

Documentation

  • Cable schedule and patching matrix
  • Route mark-ups and label register
  • Test results and defect/retest records

Standardisation

  • Agree cable IDs, endpoint notation, label format and test-result naming.
  • Keep the connection matrix and physical labels aligned with approved changes.

Proposed SOP outlines

  • Verify both endpoints and the approved route before installation.
  • Inspect/clean where applicable; connect; test; record result; resolve and retest defects.

Deliverables

  • Verified connection matrix and test evidence
  • As-built route/port records

Working basis

These are TACHOS prototype outlines to adapt and approve for each contract, not executable site procedures or a claim of standards compliance. Use current client requirements, approved designs and manufacturer instructions.

Lifecycle/documentation foundation: BICSI Essentials of Data Center Projects, 1st ed. (2019), change control p. 3-9; acceptance and closeout pp. 5-16–5-19. This is a project guide, not a current design specification.
