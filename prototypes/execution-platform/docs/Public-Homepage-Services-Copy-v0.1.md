**Status: editorial proposal, not published or approved.** Intended audience: prospective UK/European data-centre infrastructure clients.

## Headline
**From project mandate to operational handover.**

## Introductory copy
TACHOS supports structured data-centre and network-infrastructure delivery, connecting requirements, site readiness, installation, verification and handover. Our project framework brings clear responsibilities, evidence-based checkpoints and disciplined change control to complex technical work.

## Executive delivery sequence

**01 — Define and assess**  
Authorise scope and responsibilities; establish service and security requirements; survey the site and assess feasibility. *(S00–S02)*

**02 — Design and prepare**  
Coordinate technical design, establish an approved baseline, plan procurement and confirm installation readiness. *(S03–S05)*

**03 — Deliver and verify**  
Control installation and as-built records; inspect components, verify subsystems and prepare integrated testing. *(S06–S08)*

**04 — Accept and transfer**  
Support controlled cutover, service acceptance, operational documentation and accountable handover. *(S09–S10)*

**Across every stage**  
Risk, quality, security, change, commissioning and document control remain active throughout delivery.
