Project Delivery

Plan and govern the delivery.

Proposed lifecycle mapping. The hero names are retained from the artwork. These are overlapping work areas, not five mandatory sequential stages. Project Delivery spans the lifecycle; documentation starts during planning and continues throughout.

Activities

  • Confirm scope, exclusions, stakeholders and acceptance criteria.
  • Survey site readiness, access, dependencies and delivery constraints.
  • Maintain the programme, risks, issues and approved changes.

Documentation

  • Scope of work and responsibility matrix
  • Survey notes, programme and risk/issue log
  • Change register, daily progress and acceptance plan

Standardisation

  • Use a single document register with owner, revision, approval and status.
  • Agree naming, drawing formats and acceptance evidence before work starts.

Proposed SOP outlines

  • Check scope and dependencies before releasing a work package.
  • Record a proposed change; assess impact; obtain approval; update the baseline.

Deliverables

  • Approved work package and readiness decision
  • Traceable change decisions and progress records

Working basis

These are TACHOS prototype outlines to adapt and approve for each contract, not executable site procedures or a claim of standards compliance. Use current client requirements, approved designs and manufacturer instructions.

Lifecycle/documentation foundation: BICSI Essentials of Data Center Projects, 1st ed. (2019), change control p. 3-9; acceptance and closeout pp. 5-16–5-19. This is a project guide, not a current design specification.
