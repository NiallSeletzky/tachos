Documentation & Handover

Maintain evidence, accept and close.

Proposed lifecycle mapping. The hero names are retained from the artwork. These are overlapping work areas, not five mandatory sequential stages. Project Delivery spans the lifecycle; documentation starts during planning and continues throughout.

Activities

  • Capture as-built changes throughout the work.
  • Resolve snags, assemble acceptance evidence and confirm owner sign-off.
  • Transfer agreed operating information and archive the final approved pack.

Documentation

  • As-built drawings, rack elevations and patching matrix
  • Acceptance/test evidence and snag closeout
  • O&M information, warranties and handover record

Standardisation

  • Maintain document owner, revision, approval status and storage location.
  • Use agreed final formats and retain superseded revisions in the controlled archive.

Proposed SOP outlines

  • Check the deliverables register against the agreed acceptance plan.
  • Resolve gaps; obtain approval; hand over the controlled pack; record remaining obligations.

Deliverables

  • Approved handover pack and sign-off record
  • Controlled archive and outstanding-action owners

Working basis

These are TACHOS prototype outlines to adapt and approve for each contract, not executable site procedures or a claim of standards compliance. Use current client requirements, approved designs and manufacturer instructions.

Lifecycle/documentation foundation: BICSI Essentials of Data Center Projects, 1st ed. (2019), change control p. 3-9; acceptance and closeout pp. 5-16–5-19. This is a project guide, not a current design specification.
